About Us

Summit Climbing Gym was opened in 2015 by Priya and Josh, two climbers who wanted a friendly place for beginners to learn.

Today our team of qualified instructors runs beginner classes, kids' sessions and a climbing squad. Our staff check every rope and harness before the doors open each morning.
